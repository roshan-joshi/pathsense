# Partnership brief

## Who we are asking
An Australian organisation supporting people who are blind or have low vision, approached as an **expert partner** whose lived-experience and professional knowledge should shape what gets built. PathSense is not presented as a finished product or as a replacement for orientation and mobility training or rehabilitation services.

## What we are asking for
1. **Community co-design** — sessions with willing participants, starting with one person and their home.
2. **Accessibility and rehabilitation expertise** — O&M, occupational therapy and assistive-technology advice on safety limits and interaction design.
3. **Feasibility prototype testing** — a controlled comparison of current methods vs. PathSense-assisted outcomes on one real task.
4. **Funding or funding-partner support** — feasibility funding, introductions to institutional grants or funding partnerships.

## Proposed pathway
| Stage | Scope | Decision gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery and co-design | Understand the person, their home, existing methods and priorities | Agreed first task and success measures |
| First functional prototype | Limited mobile-camera object-finding and verification flow | Prototype passes safety and accessibility review |
| Controlled evaluation | Current methods vs. PathSense-assisted outcomes, six plain measures | Measurable benefit on independence, no safety failures |
| Next technical stage | Research whether a remotely controlled camera improves hands-free assistance | Only if Stage 1 shows benefit |
| Future expansion | Multiple cameras, wider household activities | Only if earlier stages justify complexity and privacy cost |

## Discussion topics
Feasibility funding · specialist input · research collaboration · access to willing participants · ethical evaluation · possible institutional grants or funding partnerships.

## Risks and mitigations to raise honestly
- **Accuracy and over-reliance** — the person verifies every result; uncertainty is spoken; existing techniques stay primary.
- **Privacy (future cameras)** — on only when asked, visibly active, user-controlled stop, approved memory, deletion, visitor protection.
- **Safety scope creep** — hot water, gas and appliances are out of scope for AI supervision.
- **Participant burden** — small, willing cohort; evaluation designed with the partner's ethics process.
