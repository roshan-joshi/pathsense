# Delivery report — PathSense Home cinematic presentation (revision v3 for review)

## What this is
A five-minute (5:17), seven-chapter cinematic web presentation proposing PathSense Home: a personalised AI task-assistance platform to be co-designed with people who are blind or have low vision. The story follows a **fictional former café owner**; scenes are AI-generated illustrations.

## What changed in this revision
- Story: a fictional former café owner replaces the earlier protagonist; no real-person details remain.
- Opening: black → room tone → sound cues → ochre bloom → the home emerges (an emergence from absence of information, not a claim about sight).
- Narration: local Kokoro neural TTS (`bf_emma`) replaces the earlier system voice.
- Australian earth palette (colour inspiration only; no cultural motifs) and warm grading.
- Audience UI cleaned: no presenter notes in the viewing window; controls auto-hide while playing.

## What is simulated
The coffee-finding flow and the "Try it" overlay (no AI or camera exists), the co-design session, the chapter 7 outcome, and all Stage 2 and 3 camera capabilities. Each carries an on-screen label.

## Known limitations
- Narration is synthesised, not a voice actor, and British-accented (no Australian neural voice was available offline).
- Manual VoiceOver / NVDA walkthroughs have not been performed.
- Only Chromium was tested.
- PDF, PowerPoint and video export scripts exist but their outputs are not part of this repository.
- Contact details on the final screen are a placeholder (`src/content/config.ts`).
